# Element EQ Privacy Summary

**Effective Date:** November 14, 2026  
**Last Updated:** November 14, 2026

Element EQ is an Android audio equalizer app developed by Anugrah Korambeth. We are committed to protecting your privacy and keeping personal data collection to a minimum.

## What We Collect

We do not intentionally collect or upload your audio recordings, voice conversations, or personal music library content.

Element EQ may access the following information only to provide app features:

- Real-time audio data while the app is actively processing sound for features like live audio adjustments and visualization.
- Audio device and Bluetooth information needed for device-aware equalizer profiles and compatible hardware detection.
- Installed app information required for per-app equalizer settings and media app selection.
- Notification information only when you explicitly enable Notification Access for app-based audio profile switching.
- Local app settings and custom equalizer presets saved on your device.
- Crash and technical diagnostic data through Firebase Crashlytics to improve app stability.

## How We Use Your Data

Element EQ processes most audio features locally on your device. Your equalizer settings, presets, and preferences are stored locally and are not normally sent to our servers.

We use only the information needed to:

- provide audio tuning and equalizer functionality,
- support real-time processing and visual feedback,
- manage device-specific and app-specific audio profiles,
- improve app stability and fix technical issues.

## Optional Google Drive Backup

If you choose to enable Google Drive backup, Element EQ may upload app-specific settings and presets to your Google Drive application data folder. This is optional and requires your explicit consent.

We do not use Google Drive backup to store microphone recordings or unrelated personal files.

## Third-Party Services

We may use the following services:

- Firebase Crashlytics for app crash diagnostics and stability monitoring.
- Google Play Services and Google privacy services as needed for Android app functionality and support.

These services may process technical information according to their own privacy policies.

## Permissions

Element EQ may request Android permissions such as microphone access, Bluetooth access, notification access, and app visibility features to support audio processing and device-aware equalizer features. These permissions are used only for the functionality they enable.

## Data Retention

- Local app settings and presets are stored on your device until you delete them or uninstall the app.
- Diagnostic data may be retained by Firebase and other providers according to their policies.
- If you use Google Drive backup, those backup files remain until you delete them or disconnect the backup feature.

## Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

We may disclose data only when required by law, necessary to protect users or the app, or to support service providers used for app functionality and security.

## Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict certain personal data. You may also withdraw consent for optional services such as Google Drive backup.
